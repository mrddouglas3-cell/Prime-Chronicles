# The Chronicles of Prime

Everybody arrives. Nobody plans to. The official hub for The Chronicles of Prime, the cities, the characters, and the stories that started it all. As Jasper would say.

A planet nine continents wide, where orcs build in the shadow of corporate spires, dead men wake up as cats, and every character has to figure out where they fit. This site is the entry point into the World of Prime, built from *The Prime Chronicles, Vol. 1: Origins*.

**Written by Dorsey Douglas III, aka XeroPrime**

## What's here

- **World** — Nocturne and New Saint, the two cities most stories begin in
- **Stories** — the seven origin tales from Vol. 1
- **Characters** — spotlight cards for the cast, filterable by city and setting

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step, no dependencies. Static and portable.
